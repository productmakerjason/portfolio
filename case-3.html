3Q Framework
PM Curriculum Design

PM Tutor · TeamSparta (Sep 2024 – Nov 2025) · Goorm (Dec 2025 – Jul 2026)

Designed a curriculum that turns non-specialists into data-informed product managers, achieving a 70% employment rate and a 90%+ completion rate.

The Problem

Tracking where mentees were failing to find employment showed the drop-off wasn't a skills gap. It was happening at the very first hurdle: CV screening.

The Approach

I built the 3Q Framework around three pillars: Direction, Metacognition, and Consistency. Practical mentoring in AI usage, AARRR, data analysis, and SQL was built into the curriculum, focused on helping mentees assess and adjust their own progress.

The Build

  • 3Q Framework: Direction, Metacognition, Consistency
  • Applied curriculum: AI usage, AARRR, data analysis, SQL
  • One-to-one mentoring: covering confidence and role fit, not just skills
