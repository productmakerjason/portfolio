Clonet
Partner Growth & Process Fix

Product Manager (Full-time) · Apr 2020 – Mar 2021

Diagnosed a chronic dev-planning bottleneck and fixed it, while personally driving partner acquisition that grew onboarded brands 5x on a 7-person fashion e-commerce team.

The Problem

The CEO's unfiltered ideas flowed straight to a single developer with no prioritisation. That developer began blocking new requests outright, stalling the roadmap.

The Approach

I introduced a filtered intake process between planning and engineering, giving the team a way to prioritise before work reached the developer. Alongside product and UX/UI duties, I personally drove partner acquisition through daily cold calls and emails.

The Build

  • Filtered intake process: sits between planning and engineering
  • Partner acquisition motion: daily cold calls and emails
  • End-to-end ownership: from first contact through to product samples and video production

The Result

25%
operational efficiency gain
5x
onboarded brands (3–5 → 25)
