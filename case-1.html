Seoul Design Project
0→1 B2B Marketplace Pivot

Product Manager (Part-time) · Jul 2023 – Jan 2025

Pivoted a design community running a £51K annual deficit into a working B2B marketplace, growing cumulative revenue to £150–200K and lifting client-side contract acceptance by 50% within 19 months.

Image placeholder

The Problem

The platform had no working business model and was running a £51K annual deficit. As part of a 6-person founding team reporting to the CEO, I inherited a community product with no clear path to revenue.

The Approach

I built "RFP Maker" to convert vague client briefs into structured project data, directly targeting the ambiguity that was blocking agencies from accepting contracts. I also replaced multi-layer client approval chains with a single empowered point of contact.

The Build

  • RFP Maker: converts vague client briefs into structured project data
  • Single point of contact: replaced multi-layer approval chains
  • Pivot sequencing: completed the core pivot in 7 months, then scaled revenue over the following 10 months, with individual contracts averaging £5K+
