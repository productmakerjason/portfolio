PM Tutor — TeamSparta & Goorm
3Q Framework, PM Curriculum Design

Product Manager Tutor (Contract) · Sep 2024 – Jul 2026

Created the "3Q" framework and scaled it across PM curriculum and career-coaching programmes, reaching 160–170 people and lifting a national government evaluation score from 3.7 to 4.2.

The Problem

Non-specialist learners needed a structured way to become credible product manager candidates, but existing mentoring had no repeatable framework connecting goals, self-assessment and consistent execution.

The Approach

I created the "3Q" framework, built around goals, strengths and weaknesses, and interests, then scaled it across TeamSparta's PM curriculum and career-coaching programmes before extending it at Goorm with an added career-storytelling track.

The Build

  • 3Q Framework: goals, strengths/weaknesses, interests
  • Scaled delivery: 2 PM cohorts (~90 learners) plus a Career Coach track (70–80 learners), reaching 160–170 people in total
  • Career-storytelling track: added at Goorm to extend the framework
